Delete Your Account

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your Bearly Invested account and the personal data associated with it. Bearly Invested is operated by Ninad Sadashiv Bhide, an individual developer. You can delete your account at any time — either from inside the app, or by contacting us if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the Bearly Invested app and sign in.
  2. Open the side menu and tap Settings.
  3. Under the “Danger zone”, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email us from the address on your account at support@bearlyinvested.app with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify your request and delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

DataDeleted
Your account and login credentialsYes — permanently
Profile information (email, name, phone number, avatar, investing-experience level)Yes — permanently
Your record of unlocked stocks and in-app preferencesYes — permanently

Deletion is immediate when done in-app. We may retain a limited amount of information only where required to meet legal obligations, prevent fraud or abuse, or resolve disputes; any such data is deleted once that need has passed.

Important — cancel your subscription separately. Deleting your account does not cancel any active Pro subscription. Subscriptions are billed and managed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and only they can cancel one. To avoid further charges, cancel your subscription in your Google Play or App Store account before deleting your account.

Questions

For anything about account or data deletion, contact support@bearlyinvested.app.
